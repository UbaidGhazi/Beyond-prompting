# 📘 COMSATS University Islamabad (CUI) Wah Campus
## Official Student Information Handbook & Knowledge Base (2026-2027)

*This official document serves as the master knowledge corpus for the CUI Wah AI Assistant, Context Engineering labs, and Mini-RAG applications.*

---

### [DOC-ACADEMIC] Academic Regulations, Attendance & Grading Policy
1. **Attendance Requirement:** Every registered undergraduate student must maintain a minimum of **80% attendance** in lectures and laboratory sessions for each course to be eligible to sit for Terminal Examinations.
2. **Attendance Penalty:** Any student with attendance below 80% is awarded an automatic **'F' grade (Short Attendance)** and must repeat the course when next offered.
3. **Medical Leave:** Medical leave applications must be endorsed by the Campus Medical Officer and submitted to the Departmental Examination Committee within **7 working days** of the missed class.
4. **Grading System:** Grading is based on a **4.00 CGPA scale**. Minimum CGPA to avoid academic warning is **2.00**. Minimum CGPA required to graduate is **2.00**.
5. **Course Withdrawal:** Students may withdraw from a course without academic penalty up to the **end of Week 10** with HOD approval.

---

### [DOC-FEES] Tuition Fees, Challan Generation & Payment Deadlines
1. **Challan Generation:** Semester fee challans are automatically generated through the CUI Student Portal at the start of each semester.
2. **Payment Methods:** Fees can be deposited at any on-campus **Allied Bank (ABL)** booth, online via Kuickpay, or 1Link mobile banking apps.
3. **Payment Deadline:** Standard fee payment deadline for Fall 2026 is **September 15, 2026**.
4. **Late Payment Surcharge:** A late fee surcharge of **PKR 100 per day** applies for the first 10 days after the due date, increasing to **PKR 2,000 flat fine** afterwards.
5. **Fee Installments:** Needy students can apply for a **2-installment fee plan** through the Student Financial Aid Office (SFAO) before the official semester start.

---

### [DOC-HOSTEL] On-Campus Hostel Accommodation & Mess Charges
1. **Hostel Facilities:** CUI Wah provides separate, secured on-campus hostel buildings for male and female students with 24/7 security and biometric entry.
2. **Room Charges:** For semester Fall 2026, hostel accommodation dues are **PKR 35,000 per semester** (covering furnished room, 24/7 electricity backup generator, and high-speed Wi-Fi).
3. **Mess Charges:** For semester Fall 2026, the monthly hostel mess charge is **PKR 14,000 per month** (or **PKR 70,000 per 5-month semester**), providing hygienic breakfast, lunch, and dinner.
4. **Curfew Timings:** Strict hostel curfew is **10:00 PM** daily. Gate closure is strictly enforced; late arrivals require warden permission.
5. **Laundry & Gym:** Free automated laundry rooms and indoor gymnasium are accessible to all registered hostel residents from 6:00 PM to 9:30 PM.

### [DOC-TRANSPORT] Campus Bus Transport Routes & Schedules
1. **Coverage Area:** CUI Wah university transport fleet operates 18 dedicated luxury buses covering **Rawalpindi, Islamabad, Wah Cantt, Taxila, Hassan Abdal, and Kamra/Attock**.
2. **Transport Fee:** The semester transport fee is **PKR 26,000 per semester** for twin cities (Rawalpindi/Islamabad) and **PKR 16,000 per semester** for local Wah Cantt/Taxila routes.
3. **Departure Timings:**
   - **Morning Inbound:** Buses depart terminal points (e.g. Saddar Rawalpindi, Zero Point Islamabad) promptly at **07:15 AM** to reach campus by 08:15 AM.
   - **Evening Outbound:** Buses depart campus parking at **04:45 PM** from Monday to Thursday, and **01:15 PM** on Friday.
4. **Bus Cards:** Student bus RFID cards are issued by the Transport Officer (Block A, Room 008) upon proof of fee submission.

### [DOC-LABS] Computing Laboratories & Equipment Guidelines
1. **Lab Locations:** CS Computer Labs 01, 02, 03, and 05 are located on the 1st and 2nd floors of **Academic Block 2**.
2. **Operating Hours:** Open Monday through Friday from **08:30 AM to 04:30 PM**.
3. **Software Configuration:** All 180 lab workstations run **Windows 11 Enterprise**, Python 3.13, VS Code, Git, Docker Desktop, and the Google GenAI SDK.
4. **Food & Drink Policy:** Strictly **no food, open drinks, or chewing gum** allowed near workstations. Water bottles must be kept sealed in backpacks.
5. **Lab In-Charge:** Engr. Farhan (Office: Block B, Room 104) oversees lab reservations, printing quotas, and hardware assistance.

### [DOC-LIBRARY] Campus Central Library & IEEE Digital Access
1. **Borrowing Entitlement:** Undergraduate students may borrow up to **3 physical books** simultaneously for a period of **14 calendar days**.
2. **Overdue Penalty:** Fines for overdue books are charged at **PKR 10 per day per book**.
3. **Book Renewals:** Books may be renewed once online via the library portal if no reservation hold exists.
4. **Digital Library Access:** Access to **IEEE Xplore**, ACM Digital Portal, and SpringerLink journals is available campus-wide through the institutional Wi-Fi without personal logins.
5. **Silent Study Areas:** The second floor is designated as a 100% silent study zone with private cubicles and laptop charging stations.

---

### [DOC-SCHOLARSHIPS] Financial Aid & Campus Merit Scholarships
1. **Merit Scholarship:** The top 5% academic performers in each semester with a **CGPA >= 3.50** are awarded **50% to 100% tuition fee waivers**.
2. **HEC Need-Based Aid:** Financially deserving students can receive full tuition coverage plus a monthly living stipend of PKR 6,000 via HEC Need-Based program.
3. **PEEF Scholarship:** Punjab Educational Endowment Fund (PEEF) scholarships are available for domicile holders of Punjab with parent income under PKR 45,000/month.
4. **Application Office:** All scholarship documents must be submitted to the Student Financial Aid Office (SFAO), Ground Floor, Administration Block.

### [DOC-HEALTH-SPORTS] Health Clinic, Cafeteria & Sports Facilities
1. **Medical Clinic:** An on-campus medical dispensary with a full-time doctor and emergency 24/7 ambulance service is located near Gate 2 (Emergency Dial: Ext 112).
2. **Cafeteria:** Central cafeteria operates from **08:00 AM to 06:00 PM**, serving subsidized meals, snacks, fresh tea, and juices.
3. **Sports Facilities:** The campus features floodlit futsal grounds, basketball courts, badminton courts, table tennis halls, and cricket practice nets. Equipment can be issued with a student ID card from the Directorate of Sports.
